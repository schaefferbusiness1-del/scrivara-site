🩺 MLS Assist

A small browser companion that turns your dictation into a finished note and drops it into any web EMR — and can pull a whole chart into MLS in one click.

Step 1 · Download

Get the extension

No Chrome Web Store needed. ~10 KB.

⬇ Download MLS Assist (.zip)

After it downloads, unzip it and remember the folder.

Step 2 · Load it into Chrome

One-time, takes about a minute

  1. Open a new tab and go to chrome://extensions
  2. Turn on Developer mode (toggle, top-right corner)
  3. Click Load unpacked (top-left) and select the unzipped mls-assist-extension folder
  4. MLS Assist now appears in your extensions. Pin it if you like.

Works in Chrome and Edge. It stays loaded after this — you only do Step 2 once.

Step 3 · Add your key

Connect it to your MLS account

  1. In MLS, go to Settings → Integrations and click Generate to create an API key (copy it).
  2. Click the MLS Assist icon in your Chrome toolbar.
  3. Paste the key, click Save. Done.
How to use it

On any EMR page

  1. Click the floating 🩺 badge (bottom-right of the page).
  2. Dictate the visit (or select text from the chart), then Generate note.
  3. Review the draft, click Insert into chart — it finds the note field for you.
  4. Or click 📋 Capture whole chart → MLS to pull the patient and their prior visits into MLS.

🔒 Your safety rail: MLS Assist never clicks Save, Sign, or Submit in the chart on its own. You review every note and approve every commit. The Autopilot pauses and asks you before anything is finalized.

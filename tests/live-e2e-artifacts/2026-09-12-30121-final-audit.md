# MLS Assist 3.0.121 pre-reload audit

Status: source repaired, tested, packaged and staged. Supersedes 3.0.120 as the
pre-reload candidate. Nothing was installed, reloaded, published or written to
Athena. Browser fixtures were synthetic local pages only.

## Evidence-backed repairs

1. AX scoped-day census: 3.0.120 returned success but terminal incomplete for a
   healthy one-in-day/three-other-day result, because expected counted every
   harvested encounter while parsed counted only the requested day. A cap was
   also mistaken for a complete scan, and unknown dates counted as other-day
   exclusions. The executable regression failed on the original candidate.
   3.0.121 validates identity before date classification, keeps unknown/missing
   encounters in the denominator, reports attempted/not-attempted accurately,
   and supplies scoped status/absence evidence. A proven empty day requires the
   account-local calendar authority. Full-history census behavior is preserved.

2. Scheduling fields: the modern reader retained only a stripped 120-character
   reason; the legacy reason helper discarded text longer than 500 characters;
   snapshot identity recovery explicitly replaced the reason with an empty
   string. Both readers now separately capture the entire exposed row-local
   reason field, including numbers and line breaks, up to 16,000 characters.
   Snapshot recovery extracts from the same immutable HTML snapshot as its
   identity proof. Oversize, truncated snapshot, changed/unbound appointment,
   conflicting fields and missing/unreadable source are explicit incomplete
   outcomes, never truncated text labeled complete. Exact appointment ID is
   required for capture and duplicate enrichment. Conflicting complete copies
   clear both the new note and the legacy reason. A later unrendered duplicate
   cannot erase a complete capture. Synthetic modern/legacy reader, real DOM
   snapshot, row churn, conflict, boundary and merge/serialization proofs pass.

3. Diagnostic privacy: encounter frame diagnostics formerly included a patient
   name and raw frame URL. Legacy schedule name-conflict diagnostics included
   both patient names. They now carry surface labels/booleans; URL-tail evidence
   uses a fixed vocabulary. Synthetic patient-name/URL canaries do not escape.

## Appointment schema for the site owner

Each supported appointment row carries `schedulingNote` and
`schedulingNoteReceipt: {version:1, source, status, complete, chars}`.
`source` is `schedule-reason-field` or `schedule-reason-snapshot`.
Successful statuses are `captured` and `empty`. Incomplete statuses are
`appointment-unbound`, `row-changed`, `source-not-rendered`, `source-unreadable`,
`snapshot-truncated`, `snapshot-unreadable`, `too-large`, `conflicting-fields`.
The existing `reason` field also receives a complete nonempty capture for
compatibility. Receipts contain no text or patient identifiers. The clinical
text remains only in the authorized appointment payload.

The site must persist and carry the two new fields by exact appointment through
the next-appointment OP-note context. A receipt with `complete:true` proves the
entire exposed reason field was captured; it does not prove another hidden
Athena detail field was available or read. Live field-to-source comparison is
still needed. Unrendered is unknown, not a declaration that the note is empty.

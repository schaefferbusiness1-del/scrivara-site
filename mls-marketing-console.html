3Review replies — draft, you approve, then publish

MLS drafts a warm, compliant reply to each recent review. You edit/approve; nothing posts until you say so. HIPAA-safe: replies never confirm someone was a patient or mention any care detail.

4Review-request campaigns

Ask patients to leave a review — the compliant way. MLS only messages patients your office already has consent to contact, one polite ask, easy opt-out, never incentivized. Off until you enable it.
Sending goes through the review-request engine in section 5 (respecting consent + opt-out). No message goes to a patient without consent.

5Auto-review from a visit

After a good visit, one tap in MLS asks the patient for a review with an editable AI starting draft — they write it in their own words and post it themselves. Never fake, never auto-posted, no incentives.

Conversion

Batch engine — ask recent completed visits

Pulls your recently completed visits, filters to patients with contact info who haven't been asked and haven't opted out, and (on your confirm) sends each a compliant request. Preview first — nothing sends until you review and confirm. Requires the Pro tier + your consent attestation below.

6Paid ads — Google Ads

Runs on your own Google Ads account. You connect it once (on Google's secure sign-in — MLS never sees your password), then MLS drafts campaigns. Every budget needs your explicit approval, and each campaign has a separate Launch step before it can spend. MLS never spends your ad money on its own.

7Payouts (optional) — Stripe Connect

If MLS ever routes a payment to your practice (a refund, a promotion, or a future revenue share), Stripe needs your payout account. You set this up yourself on Stripe's secure pages — MLS never sees your bank details and never fills anything in for you. It's the same one-time onboarding used elsewhere in MLS, so you only do it once.

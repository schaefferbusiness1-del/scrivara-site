# The op-note room — the whole flow in 6 steps

Stage 4 deliverable of OPNOTE_WORKROOM_PLAN_2026-07-26.md (owner-approved
direction: one full-screen room, Templates as its second tab, always-adaptive
templates, MLS-record auto-naming). This is the doctor-facing walkthrough —
if a step needs explaining beyond its one line, that step is a defect.

1. **Open it** — 💉 *Prep op notes* from the Visit tab (or *Draft op note* on
   any patient). The room fills the screen: patients on the left, your work
   in the middle, Templates one tab away.
2. **Pick who** — *This patient*, or *All scheduled patients* + a day
   (Today / Tomorrow / any date). Every scheduled procedure appears with the
   closest template already matched; the left rail shows one row per patient
   with a status dot (drafted · blanks left · not started).
3. **Draft** — ✨ *Draft all op notes*, or draft one row at a time. Rows you
   hand-edited are never overwritten. Each row shows an honest context line:
   how many verified visits + chart ground its draft.
4. **Fill what's missing** — every case-specific detail (needle, doses, levels)
   appears together in one **Fields** box above the note: type or dictate,
   saved defaults offer themselves first.
5. **Save** — 💾 *Save to chart*. Every draft auto-saves to History as
   **patient — date — procedure** while you work; nothing is sent anywhere.
6. **Verify** — when you choose to, route it under *Review Athena actions*
   and check it with *Verify in Athena*. MLS never writes to Athena on its
   own — the review gate stands.

Exit: the one ✕ (top right) or Esc. Esc on the Templates tab returns to
Procedures first; a second Esc leaves the room.

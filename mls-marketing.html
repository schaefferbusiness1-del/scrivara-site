3Review replies — draft, you approve, then publish

MLS drafts a warm, compliant reply to each recent review (from your scanned reviews). You edit/approve; nothing posts until you say so. HIPAA-safe: replies never confirm someone was a patient or mention any care details.

4Review-request campaigns

Ask happy patients to leave a review — the compliant way. MLS only messages patients your office already has consent to contact, one polite ask, easy opt-out, never incentivized. Off until you enable it.
Sending needs the campaign scheduler + your patient contact list (existing comms consent) wired on the server — see the hooks below. Until then this saves your settings and MLS confirms before the first send.

Questions doctors ask us

Straight answers

How does MLS get into my EMR without an integration project?

Through the free MLS Assist browser companion. You stay logged into Athena in a tab; MLS Assist reads your schedule and charts and types the finished encounter back in — no API contract, no IT project. It never clicks Save or Sign; you review and sign in the EMR.

Does it really push everything back, or just the note?

The whole encounter: the note, each ICD-10 diagnosis, the E/M level and CPT codes, and every order — placed into their proper Athena fields, not dumped as one block of text. It stops before Save/Sign so you confirm.

What is the procedure-day op-note feature?

Upload your own operative notes once. On procedure day, MLS matches each scheduled procedure to the right template and drafts a ready op report per patient, prompting you for the case-specific blanks (needle size, dose) with a dropdown of your prior values.

Does MLS upcode to inflate billing?

No — by design. It only suggests codes supported by what's documented, and is instructed never to invent findings to raise a level. It helps you stop under-coding; the provider reviews and confirms every code.

Is it HIPAA compliant?

It's a HIPAA-ready architecture — encryption, two-factor auth, access controls, audit logging, encrypted backups — with BAAs in progress. We don't claim "certification" (that doesn't exist for HIPAA). Real patient data is used only after BAAs are signed and a compliance review is complete; demos use synthetic data.

Do I need to install anything or train my staff?

The app runs in your browser and intake runs on any iPad. The only install is the free MLS Assist companion for the EMR round-trip. Onboarding is minutes, not an IT project.

Will it replace my judgment?

Never. It drafts; you decide. You edit the note, confirm the codes, and sign. You're always the final medical and coding authority.

What the award means

The MLS Doctor Award is earned, not bought. Honorees are verified against public patient reviews across the web — always separating a doctor's own reviews from their practice's — and confirmed directly with the practice before listing.

⭐ Verified patient reviewsA doctor-specific rating of 4.8★ or higher across public review platforms (Google, Healthgrades, Vitals and more), with enough reviews to be meaningful.
🩺 Doctor-specific, not practice-wideOnly reviews naming the doctor count toward the doctor's award. A practice's rating is never presented as the physician's own.
📋 Documentation excellenceHonorees use modern documentation practices so visit time is spent with the patient, not the keyboard.
✔ Practice-confirmedEvery listing is confirmed with the practice, and every profile is approved by the doctor before it appears here.

How a doctor gets listed

  1. Measure. Run the free MLS Review Finder — it gathers your reviews from across the web and shows your doctor-specific rating, separated from your practice's.
  2. Qualify. Meet the criteria above (4.8★+ doctor-specific, sufficient review volume, profile accuracy).
  3. Nominate. Send your Review Finder summary to michael@mlsscribe.com (or nominate a colleague — we'll verify with them first).
  4. Verify. MLS re-checks the public sources independently and confirms the details with your practice. Written consent from the doctor is required.
  5. Get your badge. Approved honorees appear here with a profile and receive the award badge for their website and waiting room.

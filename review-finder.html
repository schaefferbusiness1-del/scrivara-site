Review Finder & Reputation

Find every review your practice already has scattered across the web, pull it into one dashboard, and turn it into more patients — the compliant way. Part of MLS Scribe.

✅ Compliant by design: this tool finds, summarizes, links, and helps you respond to real reviews, and helps you request new reviews from real patients. It never fabricates reviews, never hides legitimate ones, and never auto-posts copied reviews to Google (that violates Google's policy and can get a listing suspended).

🩺 Who are we scanning for?

Enter the doctor + practice. Everything below auto-fills from this.

🌐 Scan across the web

Click each site to open a pre-filled search in a new tab. When you find the profile, paste its rating + review count to build your dashboard. Leave blank & hit “No profile” to flag a gap you should claim.

📊 Reputation dashboard

Live summary from what you’ve captured above.

⭐ Pull positive quotes

Paste review text (from any site). We surface the strongest positive lines for compliant testimonial use.

Only use quotes as testimonials where the source platform + the patient allow it.

💬 Respond to a tough review

Paste a negative review — get a calm, professional, HIPAA-safe response draft (no PHI, no arguing).

📣 Get more reviews (compliant)

Generate a request to send real, satisfied patients — text or email, with your Google review link. In MLS Assist this can auto-offer after a signed visit.

🗂️ Your website testimonials → more Google reviews

Have testimonials on your own site? List them here to showcase them and, compliantly, invite those same patients to post the review on Google (we never copy it there for them).

How it works & the rules

  • The widget loads in a sandboxed frame — it can't read your site, your visitors, or anything on the page. It only shows your public rating.
  • It updates automatically. When your rating changes, every embedded widget updates — no code changes needed.
  • It's PHI-free by construction: only aggregate numbers + your public business info are ever sent. Never a patient name, date, or visit detail.
  • Your widget only shows a public rating once your Best Doctors listing is live (opted-in, consent-attested, subscribed, and the directory is public). Until then it shows a tasteful "coming soon" to visitors — this preview shows you the real thing in the meantime.

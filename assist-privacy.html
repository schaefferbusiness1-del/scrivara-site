MLS Assist — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026 · Provided by MLS Scribe LLC

MLS Assist is a Chrome extension that works alongside the MLS clinical app (mlsscribe.com) to help licensed clinicians document patient visits. This policy explains what the extension accesses, why, and how the information is handled.

Single purpose

MLS Assist captures a patient visit (with the clinician's consent), drafts a clinical note, performs supervised on-screen steps that the clinician approves, and can run a scheduled backup of the clinician's patient roster from their web-based EMR into the clinician's MLS account.

What the extension accesses

  • Visit content you choose to capture — microphone audio and/or on-screen text from the active tab, only when you start a capture. This is used to produce the clinical note.
  • EMR page content — when you run the supervised autopilot or the scheduled roster backup, the extension reads content from the EMR tab you are working in to take the steps you approve or to copy your patient list into MLS.
  • Local settings — your preferences (via the browser storage API) are kept on your device.

How the information is used

Captured visit content and roster data are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the MLS backend operated by MLS Scribe LLC, solely to generate notes and sync data into your own MLS account. Note-drafting uses a third-party AI provider under a Business Associate Agreement. Data is encrypted at rest. MLS Assist does not sell or share your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it for any purpose unrelated to the single purpose above or to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Permissions, and why each is needed

  • activeTab / scripting — to read the visit and take approved steps on the EMR page you're actively using.
  • tabs — to identify the EMR tab to act on and return you to MLS.
  • storage — to remember your local settings.
  • alarms — to run the optional scheduled nightly roster backup.
  • host access — the extension supports many different web EMRs, so it can be enabled on the EMR site you use. It only reads or acts on a page when you explicitly start a capture, autopilot run, or backup.

Protected health information (HIPAA)

When used with real patient information, MLS Scribe LLC acts as a Business Associate of the clinician's practice under a signed Business Associate Agreement, and maintains administrative, physical, and technical safeguards consistent with the HIPAA Security Rule.

Data retention & deletion

Visit and roster data live in your MLS account; you can delete records from within MLS, and may request account deletion by contacting us. Local settings are removed when you uninstall the extension.

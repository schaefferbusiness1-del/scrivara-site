Evaluation safeguards

What must be verified before use

A public profile is informational only. Counsel remains responsible for independently evaluating any physician, venue requirements, conflicts, and engagement terms.

🔎

Public-release review

Unreleased, draft, synthetic, and placeholder profiles remain hidden. Public release does not itself verify professional qualifications.

🎓

Verify credentials

Any specialty, certification, experience, or CV information shown is physician supplied. Confirm it with authoritative licensing and certification sources.

📍

Confirm jurisdiction

A listed jurisdiction is not legal advice or a guarantee of eligibility for a particular matter. Check current licensure and venue rules independently.

💵

No active pricing

Any physician-supplied fee information is informational only. MLS is not offering an engagement, invoice, checkout, or payment workflow.

⏱️

Turnaround not offered

MLS is not accepting cases or promising report turnaround while the legal release and delivery controls are incomplete.

🔒

Release controls not implemented

Do not upload records or authorizations. MLS cannot verify patient authorization, sign, take payment for, or release a medical-legal report in this build.

FAQ

Questions attorneys ask

Can I submit a case or receive a report?

No. This release is limited to synthetic workflow evaluation. Case intake, patient authorization verification, signature, payment, release, and delivery are unavailable.

Are physician engagements available?

No. This evaluation does not offer matchmaking, physician engagement, medical or legal opinions, or case services for either side of a matter.

What specialties are available?

No specialty or physician is available through MLS in this evaluation release. If the directory shows a profile in the future, it means only that the profile passed public-release review; verify its statements independently.

What does it cost?

Pricing and payment are not active in this evaluation build. No report can be purchased or unlocked.

Can I request deposition or trial testimony?

No. Deposition and trial requests are not accepted. Any profile statement about experience, availability, or rates is physician supplied and is not an offer or verified engagement term.

Can I upload records or an authorization?

No. Do not upload patient records, case files, or authorizations. The release ceremony and its verification controls have not been implemented.

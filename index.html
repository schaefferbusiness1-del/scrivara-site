Security & honesty

Straight with you about what's true today

No inflated claims. Here's exactly how MLS protects data and where it stands.

🔒 HIPAA-ready architecture

Encryption in transit and at rest, two-factor authentication, automatic inactivity log-off, audit logging and encrypted backups. We say "HIPAA-ready, BAAs in progress" — not "certified" — because HIPAA certification doesn't exist.

✍️ You sign every note

AI drafts; the clinician reviews, edits and signs. The provider is always the final medical and coding authority. Nothing is submitted to your EHR without your confirmation.

🤝 BAA before any PHI

Real patient data is only used once Business Associate Agreements are signed with our AI and hosting providers and a compliance review is complete. Until then, demos use synthetic data.

MLS is a documentation and coding-support tool, not a medical device. Suggested codes are for the provider to review and confirm — final coding is the provider's responsibility, and MLS never inflates documentation to raise a bill.

FAQ

Questions doctors ask us

Do I have to switch EMRs?

No. MLS works alongside your current EMR — copy the finished note in, or, where supported, write it back automatically over HL7 FHIR R4 and SMART on FHIR. Nothing to rip and replace.

How does the athenahealth integration work?

athenahealth is live and proven today. MLS connects to athenaOne to pull your day's schedule and read chart data — problems, medications, allergies, history — so the note starts with real context, and to write the finished note back into the encounter. It stays read-only for structured data and always stops before anything is saved or signed, so the doctor reviews first.

Is there a developer API?

Yes. MLS is built on a developer-friendly REST API — generate notes, save and retrieve records, and pull schedules over clean JSON endpoints — plus FHIR keys you provision from Settings → Integrations. Talk to us if you want to build on it.

What if I don't use athenahealth?

You're covered. athenahealth works today, and for any other EMR — Epic, Cerner, eClinicalWorks, NextGen, or whatever you run — we guarantee a smart integration within 30 days of you telling us your system. Standards-based HL7 FHIR R4 and SMART on FHIR are supported too, and you can always copy the finished note into any system.

Is the AI making the diagnosis or the coding decision?

No. MLS drafts the note and suggests codes from what you documented. You review, edit, and sign — the clinician is always the final medical and coding authority, and MLS never inflates documentation to raise a bill.

Is it HIPAA compliant?

MLS uses a HIPAA-ready architecture (encryption, 2FA, audit logs, encrypted backups). Real patient data is only used after Business Associate Agreements are signed with our AI and hosting providers — until then, demos run on synthetic data. We don't claim "certified," because HIPAA certification isn't a real thing.

What is MLS Best Doctors?

It's a public directory (coming soon) where a doctor's rating is earned from de-identified, per-visit quality — never purchasable. Practices can subscribe for optional promoted placement, which is always clearly labeled and affects position only, never the star rating. It requires doctor opt-in and consent, and is in development ahead of a privacy-reviewed launch to subscribed practices.

What specialties is it built for?

MLS is tuned for spine, pain management and PM&R — the coding, intake, outcomes (ODI) and report templates reflect those workflows. It works for general visits too, but that's where it's sharpest.

How fast can I start?

Open it in your browser and generate your first note in minutes. You can bulk-import your existing patients so it's useful on day one.

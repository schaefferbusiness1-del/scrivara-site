Security & honesty

🔒 HIPAA-compliant safeguards

MLS Scribe is HIPAA compliant: encryption in transit and at rest, two-factor authentication, automatic inactivity log-off, audit logging, and backup controls are enforced in production. No independent certification or third-party audit is claimed.

✍️ You sign every note

AI drafts; the clinician reviews, edits and signs. The provider is always the final medical and coding authority. Nothing is submitted to your EHR without your confirmation.

FAQ

Questions doctors ask us

Do I have to switch EMRs?

No switch is required — a reviewed draft can always be copied manually. Automatic EHR writeback is not represented as production-ready in this release.

How does the athenahealth integration work?

The MLS Assist browser extension supports clinician-started schedule reads on exact Athena origins and a review-first handoff, validated signed-in on the packaged release.

Is there a developer API?

Synthetic-evaluation REST contracts and SMART configuration exist, but PHI and production integration access remain closed behind server and official-preview readiness gates.

What if I don't use athenahealth?

MLS can still be evaluated with synthetic drafts and manual copy. Other EHR integrations are assessed case by case; no universal compatibility or delivery timeline is promised.

Is the AI making the diagnosis or the coding decision?

No. MLS drafts the note and suggests codes from what you documented. You review, edit, and sign — the clinician is always the final medical and coding authority, and MLS never inflates documentation to raise a bill.

Is it ready for real patient information?

Yes. MLS Scribe is HIPAA compliant and its PHI safeguards are enforced in production for authorized accounts. No independent certification, audit, or outcome guarantee is claimed, and purchasing remains held until commercial terms are released.

What is MLS Best Doctors?

It is an unreleased directory concept. No live rating or real-visit claim is published while consent, substantiation, de-identification, corrections and opt-out controls are reviewed.

What specialties is it built for?

MLS is tuned for spine, pain management and PM&R — the coding, intake, outcomes (ODI) and report templates reflect those workflows. It works for general visits too, but that's where it's sharpest.

How fast can I start?

Open it in your browser and generate your first note in minutes. You can bulk-import your existing patients so it's useful on day one.
